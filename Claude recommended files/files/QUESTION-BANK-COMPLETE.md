# Marathi Corpus: Complete Question Bank
## For Data Collection (Days 1–14, Oct 1–14)

**Target**: 30–40 questions total  
**Strategy**: Balanced mix that captures natural Marathi syntax without biasing toward English speakers  
**Quality metric**: TTR 0.35–0.65, 30–100 words per response

---

## 📋 QUESTION CATEGORIES

Your form should randomize across these **4 balanced categories**:

```
CATEGORY DISTRIBUTION (per respondent):
├─ Opinion Questions (2–3 per session)     → Captures judgment syntax, modality
├─ Experience Questions (2–3 per session)  → Captures narrative, past tense, aspect
├─ Descriptive Questions (1–2 per session) → Captures adjectives, spatial language
└─ Cultural Questions (1–2 per session)    → Captures dialect markers, idioms
```

**Why these categories?**
- **Opinion**: Elicits conditional forms (`असल तर`, `हरकत नाही`, subjunctive mood)
- **Experience**: Elicits past tense, perfective aspect, narrative structure
- **Descriptive**: Elicits adjective agreement (gender-number), spatial markers
- **Cultural**: Elicits dialect-specific vocabulary, idiomatic expressions, proverbs

## 1️⃣ OPINION QUESTIONS (8–10 questions)
*Captures modality, conditionals, subjunctive mood, hedging language*

### Q1 (Easy, Personal)
```
Question (English):
"What do you think about learning English?"

Question (Marathi):
"इंग्रजी शिकण्याबद्दल तुम्हाला काय वाटतं?"

Category: Opinion
Difficulty: Easy
Estimated Time: 30–45 seconds
Min Words: 25
Max Words: 80
Tags: [opinion, education, modern-language]

Expected response pattern:
- "मला असं वाटतं की इंग्रजी शिकणं आवश्यक आहे."
- Uses: modality (वाटतं), conditional (की), necessity (आवश्यक)
```

### Q2 (Medium, Social)
```

Category: Opinion
```
